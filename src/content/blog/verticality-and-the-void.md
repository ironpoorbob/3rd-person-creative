Verticality in architecture is more than just building height—it's about the choreography of movement through space. Spiral staircases and vertical voids create dramatic spatial experiences that engage the body and the eye.

## The Poetry of Ascent

When we move vertically through a building, we experience space in a fundamentally different way. The spiral staircase, in particular, creates a continuous, flowing movement that blurs the boundaries between floors.

## Creating Drama

The void created by a central atrium or spiral staircase becomes a dramatic focal point, connecting multiple levels and creating visual relationships that wouldn't exist in a conventional floor plan.
